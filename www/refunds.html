Refunds and cancellation

Nothing to cancel in India

In India, Plus is a single payment for a month or a year. It never renews or charges you again, so there is nothing to cancel.

Cancel any time outside India

Open Me, then Manage Plus, then Cancel at the end of this period. No more payments are taken, and Plus stays on until the end of the period you already paid for. You can also cancel from the link in your Paddle receipt.

Refunds

  • If you change your mind, ask within 14 days of your first payment for a full refund, no questions asked.
  • If you were charged by mistake, for example twice, we refund the extra payment in full.
  • After 14 days, the current period is not refunded, and Plus stays on until it ends. Outside India, cancelling stops future payments.

Refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5 to 7 working days. Outside India, Paddle handles refunds as the merchant of record.

How to ask

Reply to your Razorpay or Paddle receipt, or use the help link on it, with your payment date.
