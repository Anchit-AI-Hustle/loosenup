LoosenUp for your people

Two calm minutes, SOS support one tap away, and a privacy-first view of how your people are doing.

What your members get

  • Every calm break, the Calm screen and the toolkit, free
  • Guided programmes, including Exam calm and Recovering from burnout
  • SOS with Tele-MANAS, 112 and your own counselling line
  • English and Hindi, on Android, iPhone and the web

What you see

  • An anonymous weekly dashboard: how many people shared, average mood, average Calm gain, breaks taken and the top feelings
  • A week appears only when 5 or more people shared it, so nobody can be singled out
  • Never names, notes, journal text or anything about SOS
  • Sharing is off until each member turns it on
